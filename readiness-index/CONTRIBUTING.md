# Correct or extend the index

Corrections are welcome from company teams, customers, researchers, and practitioners.

## Submit a correction

Open a [Readiness Index correction issue](../../issues/new?template=correct-readiness-index.yml) with:

1. Company and dimension.
2. Current classification.
3. Proposed classification.
4. A first-party public URL.
5. The exact passage or product behavior the source establishes.
6. Your relationship to the company, if any.

The evidence must have been public on or before the proposed effective date. Private context can inform follow-up research but will not silently change the public classification.

## Review standard

- `shown` requires explicit evidence.
- `partial` is used when an adjacent capability is public but part of the criterion remains unclear.
- `not_found_publicly` does not claim that a product lacks the capability.

Accepted corrections update the CSV, company note, and changelog in the same pull request.
