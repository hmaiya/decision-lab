1. Order total above $500: escalate to a person.
2. Customer has 3 or more refunds in the last 90 days: escalate to a person.

   - 10 or more days past the estimated delivery date: approve
   - fewer than 10 days past the estimate: request evidence (confirm the address and ask the customer to wait)

   - no photo attached: request evidence
   - photo attached and 90 days or less since delivery: approve
   - photo attached and more than 90 days since delivery: deny
5. Item is marked final sale and is not damaged or defective: deny.
6. Customer changed their mind, item unused:
   - within 30 days of delivery: approve
   - after 30 days: deny
